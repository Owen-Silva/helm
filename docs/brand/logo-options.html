Helm logo options

Five directions for Helm's own mark, replacing the ship's-wheel glyph. Each is drawn on the same 256 grid and the same graphite Windows 11 tile, then shown at 256, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px on a dark and a light ground and in the places Windows actually shows it.

Small images are the real 1x PNG renders, so on a high-DPI screen the browser enlarges them; judge pixels from the 6x zoom strip. The pick ships with pixel-hinted 16, 24 and 32 px masters; B to E are shown un-hinted (hinting would sharpen them slightly but not change the verdict).

A. Split round H

Pick

A solid disc cut by two notches reads as a bold H; a rim cut by the same notches wraps it, so the H is also a helm seen face-on (hub and spokes inside the wheel).

Why this one: it is the only direction that is both a monogram and a helm. The solid round H is ownable (nobody else's H is a cut disc) and the rim turns it into a wheel seen face-on without drawing a ship's wheel, so it reads as Helm's, not as nautical clip art. It holds up best at small sizes: at 16 and 24 px the rim would smear into the disc, so those masters drop it and keep the solid round H, hinted so every notch and crossbar edge lands on a whole pixel; the 32 px master keeps the rim with a whole 2 px gap. It is one flat shape in warm off-white on a graphite tile, with no material effects beyond a faint tile falloff and a 2 px lift shadow at 256, so it sits with Helm's grey glass and steel accent instead of competing with them, and it feels steady and deliberate, which is the "in control" you wanted the name to carry.

Wordmark: Manrope SemiBold (SIL OFL 1.1) outlined to paths, mark at 1.25x cap height. Bare mark at 20 and 28 px as the rail and welcome screen would use it. Shipped files: ui/assets/icon.svg, icon-small.svg, icon.png, icon.ico, mark.svg, wordmark.svg.

Variants of A that lost: the solid round H alone (kept for 16 and 24 px only; without the rim it reads as an emblem, not a helm), rotated 14 to 18 degrees "mid-turn" (dynamic but sporty, and an angled notch cannot be pixel-hinted), four diagonal handles (reads as a bug), an unbroken outer ring (reads as a helipad).

B. King spoke

A redrawn ship's wheel with the king spoke (the marked spoke that tells the helmsman the rudder is centred) in Helm's steel accent.

Why not: Instantly a ship's wheel, but that is the problem: it is the old clip-art idea drawn better. Below 48 px the eight handles turn into a sun or a virus, and the accent spoke vanishes at taskbar size.

C. H in the wheel

An H set inside a rim, its crossbar running out through the rim as the two side handles.

Why not: Reads as a globe (or the astronomical Earth symbol) first and an H second, at every size.

D. Heading quadrant

Compass card abstraction: a ring with its cross-hair spokes and one filled quadrant for the heading.

Why not: The most legible at 16 px, but it reads as a pie chart or a clock at quarter past, and says nothing about Helm.

E. Tiller line

A minimal line mark: rudder stock, blade and tiller arm drawn as one stroke family.

Why not: Ownable, but at small sizes it reads as a lowercase b or a music flat, and a line mark looks thin and unfinished on the tile.
